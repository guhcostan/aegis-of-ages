# Age of Empires IV — Building Stats Reference

Scope: construction statistics and rules for standard buildings and for the English and French
landmarks. History, flavour and strategy commentary are deliberately excluded.

**Source policy.** Two independent sources are used throughout:

- **AOE4W** — the [aoe4world/data](https://github.com/aoe4world/data) repository, which is generated by
  unpacking and parsing the game's own `Attrib.sga` archive (see the repository README for the
  extraction pipeline). The snapshot used here is the `main` branch as of commit
  `b2cd38222deae40ba2db18171edf494f81410c69` (2026-05-04), tagged *"Added Jin Dynasty and Season 13
  patch 16.1.9737 data"*. Values were read from
  [buildings/all.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aoe4world/data/main/buildings/all.json)
  (also served as [data.aoe4world.com/buildings/all.json](https://data.aoe4world.com/buildings/all.json)).
- **FANDOM** — the [Age of Empires Wiki](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Building_(Age_of_Empires_IV)),
  read through the MediaWiki API. Fandom infoboxes carry `{{tt|current|previous}}` annotations that record
  the patch in which a value changed; where present these are reproduced below because they explain
  disagreements with AOE4W.

Where the two disagree, **both values are given** and the newer one is identified. Anything neither
source documents is marked **UNVERIFIED** rather than guessed.

**Conventions used in the tables.**
Ages are the standard four: Dark (I), Feudal (II), Castle (III), Imperial (IV). In both sources the
`age` field of a building is the age in which the building can be *constructed*; for landmarks the
Fandom `Use` field states the age that constructing the landmark *advances you into*.
Armour is written as `pierce / fire`. Costs and times are the generic (non-civilisation) values; the
per-civilisation deviations that matter are listed in *Special rules*.

[`src/types/items.ts`](https://github.com/aoe4world/data/blob/main/src/types/items.ts) has no size
property). The 4×4 Town Center footprint is corroborated by AOE4W's internal
`land_food_deposit_4_by_4` class on Town Centers and Capital Town Centers, and the 2×2 by
`land_food_deposit_2_by_2` on other drop-off buildings.

All twelve landmarks have 50 pierce armour. Fire armour is 0 except for The White Tower, Berkshire
Palace and Red Palace, which have 6. A landmark is not selected from the normal build menu: it is
placed as the age-up action, then constructed by a Villager, and its completion advances the age.
AOE4W corroborates this — every landmark record has `producedBy: ["villager"]` and an
`age<N>_landmark` class (`age1_landmark*` for the Dark Age pair, `age2_landmark*` for the Feudal pair,
`age3_landmark*` for the Castle pair). The "cost" column is therefore the age-up cost and the
"build time" is the age-up duration.

## Conflicts and gaps

### Confirmed conflicts between sources

1. **Wonder cost (material).** AOE4W stores a flat 5,000 of each of food, wood, stone and gold
   (20,000 total) for the Imperial-Age wonders of every civilisation except the Mongols/Golden Horde
   (see item 2). Fandom's [Wonder](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Wonder_(Age_of_Empires_IV))
   article states the cost is *variable with map size* since update 9.1.176 and lists 5,000 (Micro),
   6,000 (Small), 7,000 (Medium) and 8,000 (Large and Gigantic) of each resource — i.e. 20,000 to
   32,000 total. **The Fandom values are the newer and more granular ones**; AOE4W's flat 5,000 appears
   to be a single base value rather than a map-size-dependent one. Use the Fandom table for gameplay
   fidelity and AOE4W's figure only as the floor of the range.
2. **Mongol/Golden Horde wonder.** AOE4W gives Monument of the Great Khan 19,950 total spent as
   6,650 food, 6,650 wood and 6,650 gold with **no stone** — a genuine per-civilisation deviation from
   the 5,000-each pattern. Fandom's cost table does not break this out.
3. **Siege Workshop cost.** AOE4W and Fandom both give the current **250 wood**; Fandom additionally
   records **300 wood before patch 12.1.2454**. 250 is newer.
4. **Market build time.** Both give **20 s**; Fandom records **30 s before the Season Three Update**.
   20 is newer.
5. **Outpost HP.** Both give **750**; Fandom records **1,000 before patch 20249**. 750 is newer.
6. **Palisade Wall / Palisade Gate HP.** Both give **1,350**; Fandom records **1,250 before update
   16.1.9737** and **1,500 before Season Four**. 1,350 is newer. Palisade Wall build time 8 s, with
   **4 s before update 12.0.1974**.
7. **Town Center stone cost.** Both give **300 stone**; Fandom records **350 stone between Season
   Three and Season Thirteen**. 300 is newer, but note that 300 was also the pre-Season-Three value,
   so the cost was raised and then reverted.
8. **Capital Town Center garrison.** AOE4W gives **15**; Fandom gives **15 with 20 before patch
   20249**. 15 is newer. The same 20 → 15 reduction applies to The White Tower.
9. **Keep cost and build time.** Both give **900 stone / 180 s**; Fandom records **800 stone before
   patch 7.1.113** and **140 s before Season Three** / **120 s before Season One**.
10. **Stone Wall segment cost.** Both give **25 stone**; Fandom records **20 before update 12.0.1974**
    and **15 before Server-Side Patch 5.1.148.1**. Historic values are given in descending recency
    because the wall was re-costed more than once.
11. **English Mill influence gather bonus (internal Fandom disagreement, not a table value).** The
    [Mill](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Mill_(Age_of_Empires_IV)) article states 15%/20%/25%/30%
    per Age, while the [English](https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/English) article states
    20%/25%/30%/30% and dates the change to **update 14.0.4963**, making the English article's figures
    the newer ones. Table 1 deliberately records only that the influence exists, without a rate.

### Gaps — not confirmed by either source

- **Wall and gate footprints are UNVERIFIED.** Neither AOE4W (which emits no size field) nor the
  Fandom infoboxes for Palisade Wall, Palisade Gate, Stone Wall or Stone Wall Gate state a tile
  footprint. These are the only four rows in Table 1 with an unresolved footprint.
- **AOE4W game-version pinning is indirect.** The repository commit message names *Season 13 patch
  16.1.9737*, but the emitted JSON carries only `"__version__": "0.0.2"` (a schema version), not a game
  patch number. Individual values therefore cannot be tied to a patch from the JSON alone.
- **`Town Center` "Pop provided = +10"** comes from the Fandom `Pop` infobox field for Town Center and
  Capital Town Center. AOE4W's `Building` type declares an optional `popcapIncrease` field, but the
  generated files contain **zero** occurrences of it, so AOE4W cannot corroborate any population value
  — including the House's +10.
- **Fandom `Age` field semantics for the Town Center.** Fandom lists the Town Center as *Feudal Age*
  (it can only be built from Feudal onward) while AOE4W's `age` field is 1 (the starting Capital Town
  Center exists in Dark Age). The two are not contradictory but the column in Table 1 resolves them
  explicitly for this reason.
- **Farmer/Civilisation coverage is not exhaustive.** AOE4W contains 665 building records across 165
  distinct `baseId`s and 22+ civilisation codes. Table 1 lists the generic value plus the deviations
  found for Chinese, Order of the Dragon, Ottomans, French, English, Japanese, Malians, Mongols and
  Golden Horde; other civilisations may have deviations that were not enumerated here.
- **Wonder build time under map-size scaling.** The 600 s build time is agreed by both sources for the
  standard case, but neither source states whether any map-size variant changes it.
- **Capital Town Center availability for Knights Templar.** Fandom says Capital Town Center is
  available to all except Knights Templar; AOE4W instead carries a separate `fortress` building for
  that civilisation with 8,000 HP and 5 fire armour. The substitution is consistent across both
  sources but the exact in-game labelling of that structure is not confirmed.
- **Rus wall variants.** Fortified Palisade Wall (2,700 HP) and Fortified Palisade Gate (2,700 HP) are
  documented on Fandom as Rus-only replacements, but their footprints are likewise UNVERIFIED.

### Update-procedure note

To refresh this document, re-fetch
[buildings/all.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aoe4world/data/main/buildings/all.json) and diff
the `costs`, `hitpoints`, `armor` and `garrison` fields for the relevant `baseId`s against the values
above, then re-read the Fandom infoboxes for any `{{tt|...}}` annotation that has changed. A changed
`{{tt}}` marker is the strongest available signal that a patch altered a value.
